Before You Enroll

Questions.

Does this replace my required supervision?

No — and it never claims to. Your state-required supervision hours must be completed with a licensed supervisor. This program equips you to use those hours more strategically.

I just started my first job. Is it too early?

It's exactly the right time. This program was built for the counselor who is in their first job, not looking back on it. Every module addresses something you are facing right now.

What if I'm not a person of faith?

The First Chair™ is clinically grounded first. Faith integration appears where relevant, and The Daily Chair's faith content can be turned off entirely. Every counselor will find the curriculum applicable.

What licensure tracks does this apply to?

The content is designed for LPC, LMHC, LADC, and related mental health licensure tracks. The clinical foundations apply across settings and licensing bodies.

How long do I have access?

Lifetime. Once you enroll, the modules, workbooks, and video content are yours — including any curriculum updates made after your enrollment date.

Can I upgrade my tier after enrolling?

Yes. Start in Foundation and upgrade anytime by paying the difference. Email arieshahelps@gmail.com and we'll make it seamless.
